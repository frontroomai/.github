**The restaurant till and accounting system, with AI that does the busywork.**

Frontroom gives independent restaurants and cafes one system for the till, kitchen screen, stock, purchases, staff, payroll, VAT and banks, so the books stay current every day.

- **Till and kitchen screen:** tables, split bills and payments, and the till keeps selling when the internet drops.
- **Stock and recipes:** every sale uses up its recipe, so food cost stays current.
- **Books that keep up:** supplier bills, wages, tax and bank payments land as they happen.
- **AI cameras:** how busy the floor is, when staff arrive and leave, how active the kitchen is.
- **Ask:** type a question about your numbers and see the figures behind the answer.
- **Photo reading and voice notes:** snap an invoice or record a short note instead of typing.
